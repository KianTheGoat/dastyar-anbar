# Legal notice drafting references

The Persian privacy, cookie and terms notices are original product-specific text.
They describe implemented behavior and use runtime operator settings. No other
site's identity, tracking practices, retention, payment obligations or legal
certification has been copied into the service.

Reference structures consulted on 2026-10-12:

- Automattic privacy policy, https://automattic.com/privacy/
- WordPress.org privacy notice, https://wordpress.org/about/privacy/
- ICO cookie guidance (UK-specific reference),
  https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/privacy-notices-and-cookies/cookies-and-privacy-notices-in-detail/

These are references, not statements that those companies operate this product.
No verbatim policy text has been incorporated. The production operator must set
identity/contact, country, hosting region and subprocessors before accepting users.
